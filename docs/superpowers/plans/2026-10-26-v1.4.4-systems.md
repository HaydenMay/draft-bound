- Replace independent target field positions with 10–14 team possessions, shared field position, downs, first downs and bounded plays. Receiver routes consume real team passing plays. Receiving and other team points derive from those possessions. Long receptions can end short of the goal line. Catch difficulty and opponent strength affect catch success. Grade efficiency and production without routinely saturating100.
- Keep receiver identities, uniform team/receiver sampling, grades, ceilings, three shared rerolls, starting58–73/Gift94, eight-unit practice and school continuity. Test stronger late mastery for non-S ceilings: the last14 points use .35 rather than .5, the final4 use .125 rather than .25. S99 retains the current mastery path. Tune only against measured outcome distributions and retain a conditional99 witness.
- A fixed seeded retirement-age window makes entering at22 versus23 a real tradeoff. Returning still develops college ability and can improve draft position; declaring buys an earlier professional season. No invented NFL attribute development, injuries, forced declaration or negative growth. Legacy professional models stay unchanged.
- New prospects automatically simulate their NFL careers when their draft result is finalized. Draft night remains visible before the user opens the career recap. One canonical career-card customization is exposed, with all earned college/draft/pro moments. Preserve old two-edition entries and deep links. Keep the existing storage shape's draft snapshot/card compatibility fields rather than destructively migrating old cards.
- No pack opening, randomized cosmetic rewards, autograph implementation or new scene art.
