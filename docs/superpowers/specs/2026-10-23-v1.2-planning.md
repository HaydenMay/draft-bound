## Confirmed decisions

### Player offers

- Every displayed three-player offer must contain three different source players.
- The same source player may appear again in a later offer or reroll.
- This applies to opening gifts as well as normal potential offers.
- Normal offer sampling remains uniform among eligible players, without replacement within that offer. Reset the eligible pool between offers. No rarity weights, superstar weighting, pity systems or account bonuses.
- Opening gifts retain three distinct attributes, S-tier eligibility and distinct source players. Validate that the eligible source catalog can actually satisfy these constraints; never silently reintroduce duplicates.

### Progression and Hall of Fame

- Leave Hall of Fame qualification alone for now.
- Investigate and tune the ease of reaching 90+ college OVR. The user's six Hall of Fame careers are a symptom to investigate, not authorization for a new induction gate.
- Attribute ceilings remain distinct from current ability. Better source grades can raise a ceiling without making that ceiling easy to reach.
- Preserve the elite opening gift, genuinely uniform source rolls, four-game training blocks and cosmetic-only account progression.
- Do not change existing school names or individually rebalance schools while new programs are being considered.
- Preserve existing completed careers and earned Hall of Fame records.

### Recruiting and source grades

- Assess the finished prospect before school choice and show a recruit-star rating.
- The user chose a potential-heavy blend of starting ability and inherited potential. Exact weights and star bands are still draft defaults to decide, not approved release numbers.
- Audit source grades, starting with Andre Johnson, so signature strengths of elite historical receivers are represented appropriately. Do not turn every attribute of every Hall of Famer into an S.
- These are original game grades, not official scouting ratings. Frozen traits in existing saves retain their original values.

### Reveals, animation and simulation

- After the third manual potential, visibly reveal each automatically assigned attribute, source player, grade and ceiling individually.
- Let users see what they receive without explaining the entire mechanic again.
- Add more anticipation and motion to opening gifts, size rolls, source offers, rerolls and automatic reveals.
- Introduce quick broadcasts of actual saved game outcomes and highlights rather than only jumping to completed results.
- Keep four-game training decisions. Presentation timing never determines outcomes or consumes gameplay RNG.
- Include skip/fast controls, keyboard access, reduced motion and refresh safety. Exact pace and broadcast detail remain planning questions.

### Coaching

- Check both actual recent performance and current skills before prescribing corrective work.
- A 94-hands receiver should not routinely get JUGS as though hands were a major weakness.
- Add relevant coaching opportunities beyond JUGS; base each on meaningful samples, a relevant weakness and available ceiling headroom.
- Avoid JUGS always winning a fixed priority list.
- Preserve existing practice budgets, fulfillment rules and protection against repeat prompts. Exact trigger cutoffs and selection ranking remain draft tuning choices.

### Responsive layout

- Fix college presentation at intermediate window widths: complete labels, no collisions with meters or values, and a usable responsive grid.

### Quick and Full creation modes

- The user proposes Quick mode for the current creation flow and Full mode for control of all potentials. These are creation-mode proposals, not different college block lengths or animation settings.
- Current Quick flow: choose one natural gift, accept/roll size, manually assign three additional potentials, then automatically fill the remaining five WR attributes.
- Full should remove forced automatic assignment of the remaining potentials. Still to settle: does the user control every remaining attribute through the same random offers, or choose directly from the entire source catalog? Also settle whether the natural gift remains unchanged and what reroll budget is appropriate.
- Recommended starting interpretation, not approved: retain the natural gift and size flow, then manually choose all eight remaining potentials from three-player offers. Preserve uniform offers, current-ability-versus-ceiling distinction and duplicates only between separate offers.
- Before implementation, specify mode selection and persistence, legacy-save behavior, manual-count assumptions, reroll rules and comparison/balance reporting. Do not add a direct catalog selector, unlimited rerolls, separate rewards or a mid-creation mode switch without an explicit design decision.

## Questions to settle next

1. What should a typical competent college run finish at, and how rare should 90+ be?
2. Which levers should make reaching a ceiling harder: growth rate, diminishing returns, participation/experience, training efficiency or their combination?
3. How should starting readiness and upside map to recruit stars without making almost every build a four- or five-star recruit?
4. What should a quick broadcast show, and how long should a four-game block take to watch?
5. Which additional coaching moments should feel corrective versus optional opportunities to develop an existing strength?
