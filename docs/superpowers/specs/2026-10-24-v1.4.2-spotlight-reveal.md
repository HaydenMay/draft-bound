# Draftbound v1.4.2 — Spotlight Reveal

## Goal

Make the existing receiver-potential selection feel like a lively football game-show reveal by spotlighting the already-selected NFL team and then that team's already-selected eligible receiver.

## User-facing sequence

1. During a manual potential pick, show all 32 NFL teams in responsive text tiles. Move one bright spotlight between nonsequential tiles, then land on the selected team and show its full name.
2. Transition to a board containing only eligible receivers for that team. Use receiver names as tiles; no portraits or fabricated/filler players. Spotlight the preselected receiver and reveal the existing receiver/potential screen.
3. Keep the sequence short: approximately 1.2 seconds for the team stage, 1.0 seconds for the receiver stage, and a 0.3-second winner hold. Timings must be centralized and easy to tune.
4. Provide Skip / Reveal Now throughout. Skip immediately ends presentation on the same preselected receiver. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by bypassing the animated sequence and showing the existing readable result.

## Existing game rules and mode behavior

- Presentation only: the team and receiver are selected by existing domain logic before animation. The spotlight path must not use or mutate game RNG, change results, or grant rerolls.
- Animate only actual manual receiver picks: 3 in Quick mode and 8 in Full mode. Quick mode's automatically assigned remaining potentials are unchanged and do not launch a spotlight.
- Preserve the existing attribute selection, potential ceiling, save format, player grades, eligibility rules, and all other gameplay.
- The 32-team receiver catalog provides names and abbreviations, but has no logo or team-color assets. Use existing text identities and do not add, hotlink, or invent marks/assets.
- If the selected team has one eligible receiver, reveal that player directly without a false competition. An empty pool remains a catalog integrity error.

## Interaction and accessibility

- Use a reusable spotlight presentation for both stages, with one clearly illuminated tile at a time, nonsequential jumps, and a slower cadence near the known winner.
- Avoid layout shifts, full-screen flashes, excessive camera shake, sound dependencies, and animation that obscures the game controls or result.
- Keep the skip control keyboard and touch accessible; announce completed team/player results without announcing each rapid highlight.
- Cancel timers on skip, completion, stage changes, or unmount; prevent duplicate completion callbacks.
- Keep boards legible at narrow widths and in every theme. Spotlight must be distinguishable by border, brightness and scale, not color alone.

## Verification

- Test the decorative path independently from game outcome and verify it always ends on the specified winner.
- Browser-test the actual team and receiver boards, selected result, skip, reduced motion, Quick/Full progression, and mobile layout.
- No domain selection rules or save migration are expected to change.
