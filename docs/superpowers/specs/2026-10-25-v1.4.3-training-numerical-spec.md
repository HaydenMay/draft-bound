# v1.4.3 proposed training specification

Status: approved by the owner in chat on October 25, 2026: “Perfect. That’s what i was remembering. Feel free to implement” after reviewing the transfer factors. The approval checkpoint in the [owner brief](2026-10-25-v1.4.3-training-owner-brief.md) is satisfied for this specification. This refines candidate A from the [training audit](../../validation/V1_4_3_TRAINING_REVIEW.md).

## Intended result

Prospects start college more capable, grow less abruptly as freshmen, and retain exceptional development possibilities. Similar junior OVR is intentional for this pass. This does not resolve overall elite-potential frequency, the benefit of always returning for junior year, or unrealistic college receiving statistics. Simulation changes follow as a separate specification.

## Starting ability and ceilings

For new v1.4.3 prospects, each non-gift ability rolls an integer from 58 through 73 inclusive, clipped to its stored ceiling. This replaces 50–65 without adding random draws. The Natural Gift still starts at 94 with its existing S ceiling of 99. Keep existing attribute weights, source grades, ceiling mapping, creation choices and three shared rerolls.

These are current abilities. A borrowed S potential does not grant 99 current ability. Existing prospects must never be reinitialized or given the eight-point uplift.

## Practice and experience budgets

Practice becomes **8 work units per game**, replacing 7.2. These are inputs to diminishing returns, not guaranteed rating gains.

| Requested plan | Selected attribute | Each other attribute | Total practice |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Balanced | 8/9 on every attribute | 8/9 | 8 |
| Focused | 4.8 | 0.4 on each of eight | 8 |
| Intensive | 8 | 0 | 8 |

Experience remains `3.6 × clamp(routes / 30, 0, 1.2)`, at most 4.32 work units per game. Half is distributed equally across nine attributes; half follows existing relevant rep shares. With no routes, experience is zero but practice remains available. Keep speed, acceleration, route running and awareness mapped to routes; hands to targets; release to press reps; vertical/body control to contested targets; and after catch to receptions.

The small practice increase preserves exceptional development after replacing the old freshman multiplier. It is a refinement of the audited 7.2-budget candidate, not a previously approved constant. Specialization moves practice toward the target; it does not prevent other skills learning from games. Retargeting a capped skill remains a sensible choice. No practice overflow is redistributed automatically.

## Annual learning and school continuity

Replace the current year factors `[1.5, 1.15, 0.85]` with base factors `[0.85, 1.00, 1.00]`. Multiply by the school-history efficiency below exactly once. The previously discussed 100/95/90 sequence refers to annual school continuity in this proposal, not changing training attributes.

| School path, freshman to junior | Freshman efficiency | Sophomore efficiency | Junior efficiency |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| A → A → A | 100% | 95% | 90% |
| A → B → B | 100% | 85% | 80% |
| A → A → B | 100% | 95% | 75% |
| A → B → C | 100% | 85% | 65% |
| A → B → A | 100% | 85% | 65% |

Staying all three years therefore produces net factors `[0.85, 0.95, 0.90]`. Returning to a former school does not erase transfers. The factor is fixed within each season and applies to future development only; switching schools never removes current ability or changes stored potential.

For each attribute, combine practice and experience work, multiply by the net annual factor and that school's applicable specialty multiplier, then apply existing modern ceiling-relative mastery integration and ceiling clipping. Do not also multiply by the old year factors. Maintain proportional practice/experience attribution after diminishing returns.

School specialties remain as implemented: Meridian 1.25 for routes/release, Cinder Ridge 1.25 for speed/vertical, Openwater 1.05 for all nine. Existing opportunity modifiers still affect game opportunities. No new school ceilings, recruiting system, or offense-modifier behavior is included.

Training-priority continuity is a separate future design question. This specification adds no focus-switch penalty. A forced coaching intervention must never count as a player changing focus.

## Temporary coaching intervention

Keep current detection limits: at most two interventions per season, at most once per type per season, following eligible completed blocks. An intervention overrides only the first game of the following four-game block. Games two through four resume the submitted player plan.

Proposed interface correction: remove the redundant “Use coach’s focus” shortcut that currently turns the temporary recommendation into a persistent Intensive selection. Keep the intervention explanation and normal next-block controls. Explicitly choosing Intensive through the ordinary controls remains a persistent player choice. The correction should make the one-game duration clear.

## Measured effects and limitations

The calibration runs 57,600 scenario careers, or 2,073,600 simulated games, plus baseline parity checks. These are repeated conditions on 400 original actual builds, 400 separate validation builds, and 600 synthetic profiles; they are not 57,600 independently created prospects. The separate cohort uses independent seeds but was inspected during prior audits and reused for continuity testing; it is not an untouched final holdout. Six policies, three schools, both creation modes and automatic coaching on/off are covered by the main grid. All 27 three-school paths are tested separately with Balanced and adaptive Focused policies.

For the main 200 Full-mode builds at Openwater using Balanced requests and automatic one-game interventions:

| Checkpoint | Current rules mean OVR | Proposed mean OVR |
|---|---:|---:|
| Starting college | 63.24 | 70.01 |
| End freshman | 79.36 | 79.14 |
| End sophomore | 86.23 | 85.63 |
| End junior | 88.73 | 88.75 |

Freshman growth falls from about 16.12 to 9.14 raw OVR. Quick-mode junior mean is 88.35 under the proposal versus 88.34 currently. Independent held-out Full/Openwater/Balanced junior means are 88.815 proposed and 88.814 baseline.

This is not a solution to frequent 90+ builds: the main Full/Balanced sample moves from 34.5% to 35.5% displayed 90+, and the held-out sample from 38.5% to 39%. Non-gift attributes within one point of their ceilings remain common (59.5% proposed versus 61.125% baseline in the main Full cohort). Low-ceiling synthetic builds fully realize their smaller potentials. Address broad elite ceilings and selection incentives in the separate long-term balance work.

Opportunity costs remain visible: proposed Full/Openwater junior means are 88.75 Balanced, 86.27 Focused permanently on one attribute, and 82.64 Intensive permanently on one attribute. The other non-gift attributes gain about 21.10, 17.12 and 11.68 points respectively. These fixed-focus policies deliberately continue after their target caps; they demonstrate allocation costs, not optimal player strategy. Adaptive Focused reaches 89.35.

All-S synthetic profiles with variable 58–73 starting rolls reach mean 95.78 Balanced and 96.50 adaptive Focused. Those profiles have hand-authored ceilings; they do not estimate the chance of assembling such a prospect through creation.

A separate development-only witness reaches raw **98.5495854545**, displayed **99**, with all ceilings 99, all eight non-gift starts 73, a route-running Gift starting 94, Openwater continuity, and high fixed reps. Nine block plans are documented in the [witness JSON](../../validation/V1_4_3_TRAINING_SPEC_WITNESS.json). This proves conditional development feasibility, not an end-to-end generated career seed, realistic game opportunities, or a measured rarity. No generated 99 career has been established. A later simulation pass may change rep availability and must rerun this check.

Evidence: [calibration JSON](../../validation/V1_4_3_TRAINING_SPEC_CALIBRATION.json), [tables](../../validation/V1_4_3_TRAINING_SPEC_TABLES.md), `tools/audit-v1.4.3-training.ts --spec-calibration`, and `tools/verify-v1.4.3-training-witness.ts`. Twenty-four baseline cases match production prospect ratings, complete histories/gains, interventions and final RNG exactly. Current scoring defects remain in the simulation; its yardage/TD outputs do not establish realism.

## Implementation boundary after approval

1. Add a v1.4.3 balance-version discriminator for new runs. Missing, legacy and v1.2 values retain their existing numerical behavior. Preserve import/export compatibility without an unnecessary save-schema migration.
2. Route modern mastery integration explicitly for v1.4.3; the current `version === 'v1.2'` checks must not accidentally send it to legacy mastery. Stamp new draft metadata with the new balance version while retaining receiver catalog and pro-model identities.
3. Derive annual school history from recorded years and current college. Apply the new initialization and resource/year factors only to new-version runs. Keep deterministic random draw counts and existing four-game simulate-then-develop chronology.
4. Correct temporary-coach controls and explanatory text. Preserve stored results, legacy development and all frozen cards.
5. Verify then report results before release: old-save fixtures; budget totals; zero-route practice; ceiling bounds and nonnegative gains; practice/experience attribution; all five continuity patterns; all three specialties; temporary coaching/resumption and season limits; deterministic block chronology; new-version save round trips; regression tests; production build; and phone usability of the changed control.
6. Repeat paired distributions and the exceptional-development witness against the actual implementation. Treat rarity as unmeasured until an end-to-end creation-and-career search supports it. Do not describe a conditional witness as a frequency guarantee.

Implement only this approved numerical scope. College field-position/scoring realism, game-grade differentiation, training-focus switching rules, early declaration incentives and global potential rarity remain separate work. Artwork, extra playable positions, source regrading, injuries and playable professional training are outside this pass.
