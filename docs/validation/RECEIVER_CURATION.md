# Receiver curation and spotlight fit

Catalog: wr-curated-10-v1.4.2. 299 unique profiles across 320 memberships, ten per team. All 498 original profiles and their grades remain archived.

The owner requested recognizable names without turning every result into a star. Lists favor established names, historical starters, specialists and memorable disappointments, with examples including Titus Young, John Ross, Stephen Hill, Kevin White, Jalen Reagor, J'Mon Moore and A.J. Jenkins. This is a product curation decision, not a new factual verification of the provisional 2026 roster or scouting grades. All affiliations come from the existing snapshot. Deferred names can be reviewed again; no profile was deleted.

## Sampling

20,000 engine-built prospects, seeds 500001..520000 inclusive, alternating Quick/Full, gift index zero, first open attribute, no rerolls. Controlled experiment, not telemetry.

160,000 assignments: 110,000 manual and 50,000 automatic. Unseen eligible memberships: 0. Each team has expected 3.125% share; each of its receivers has expected 0.3125% unconditional share per draw. Players retained under several teams can appear more often overall.

## Grade review

No individual grades were modified or silently approved. Existing provenance remains attached to each profile. Team-weighted mean potential ceiling across all nine attributes: 85.9987 before; 87.0229 after. This measures offered profiles, not optimal player choices or developed OVR. Recognizability and balanced grades remain distinct concerns; any ceiling inflation or weak grade confidence belongs in the later grading/balance review.

## Compatibility and presentation

New runs pin their receiver catalog, including gifts, rerolls, manual picks and Quick autofill. Runs without that field keep the original ordered pools; their draft snapshot content version remains original. Archived boards retain all old candidates and may scroll manually. Fresh boards show two columns on phones and five on wider screens without automatic winner scrolling. Short/zoomed windows can still scroll manually rather than lose content.
