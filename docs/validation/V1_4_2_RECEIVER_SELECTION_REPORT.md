# v1.4.2 receiver selection audit

**Result:** 100,000 seeded Full-mode builds were created and advanced through the production `createRun` and `transition` functions. The sample produced 800,000 team-first receiver rolls and 900,000 graded traits. The script is reproducible with `npm run audit:v1.4.2`; set `V142_RUNS` only when intentionally running a smaller smoke sample.

## Selection policy and limits

Each build selects the highest-weight Natural Gift among the three generated gifts. On each manual pick, the simulation takes the greatest available `WR attribute weight × grade ceiling` from the single receiver revealed by the uniformly rolled team-first selection. It uses Full mode to isolate the eight visible user-choice rolls. This is a deliberately strong, scripted policy, not observed player behavior. Initial ratings remain generated by the normal production RNG.

The output describes pre-college initial OVR and the weighted potential ceiling. It does not simulate college training, declare timing, draft outcomes, professional seasons, or Hall of Fame rates. Potential OVR is a ceiling summary, not a promise that the player reaches it.

| Mean | 62.99 | 88.82 |

| Median | 63 | 89 |
| P75 | 64 | 90 |
| P90 | 65 | 91 |
| Observed range | 55–69 | 80–96 |

The most favorable visible pick policy still averaged below a 90 ceiling OVR; 25% of builds reached at least 90 at the 75th percentile, while the 90th percentile was 91. The sample did not produce a 99 ceiling OVR. This helps quantify the receiver catalog, but does not by itself set final game difficulty because users may choose differently and training changes current ratings.

## Roll sampler check

Every team was selected between 3.09% and 3.16% of the 800,000 rolls; the expected share is 3.125%. Conditional on team, the receiver is sampled uniformly from that team's eligible memberships. Across 521 eligible team/receiver memberships, observed counts ranged from 1,241 to 1,845. Expected per-member counts range from about 1,316 in a 19-player pool to about 1,786 in a 14-player pool. These frequencies are consistent with the intended two-stage sampler; source-profile chance still varies by how many eligible team pools include that player.

## Data and publication caveats

The imported source is explicitly provisional: 498 profiles, 32 teams and 521 eligible memberships. Of those profiles, 410 grading records remain marked `needs_manual_verification`, while 88 are calibration anchors. The data audit is in [V1_4_2_CATALOG_AUDIT.json](V1_4_2_CATALOG_AUDIT.json). These are game interpretations, not official evaluations or endorsements.

Real professional team names and real-player references now appear in the gameplay source flow. Review the [legal release checkpoint](../LEGAL_REVIEW_CHECKPOINT.md) before publishing. This implementation does not add team logos or marks.
