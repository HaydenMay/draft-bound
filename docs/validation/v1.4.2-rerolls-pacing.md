# v1.4.2 rerolls and spotlight pacing follow-up

Owner-requested refinement, October 24, 2026.

## Behavior

- Restore access to the existing three shared creation rerolls in both Quick and Full modes. Physical-build rerolls and receiver rerolls spend the same saved budget.
- A receiver reroll draws and saves a new team/receiver pair with its deterministic RNG checkpoint, then replays the spotlight. Previously locked traits, accepted physical build, and manual-pick progress remain intact. Independent draws may repeat a receiver.
- Disable reroll at zero remaining. Replayed command IDs do not spend another reroll. Refresh retains the current result and remaining budget. Existing saves need no migration or budget reset.
- Slow the team movement to 1.8 seconds, pause on its full name for 0.6 seconds, run receiver movement for 2.0 seconds, and pause on its name for 1.2 seconds: 5.6 seconds total. A team with a single eligible receiver bypasses receiver movement.
- Show winning names prominently in the spotlight heading. Bring the winning receiver tile into view on a scrolling board. Skip, Fast Reveal, reduced motion, keyboard focus, and timer cleanup remain supported.
