# Original Draft Bound illustrations

Six original production illustrations generated with OpenAI's image generation tool for this project, October 23, 2026. Fictional adult football athletes, unbranded uniforms and original scenes; no player photos or third-party team logos were used.

- journey.png: stadium entrance
- breakaway.png: long touchdown run
- spectacular.png: contested catch
- draft.png: draft celebration
- awards.png: individual award celebration
- hall-of-fame.png: bronze bust and gold jacket

Scenes may share art. Eligibility derives from saved game evidence, not from image appearance. Current uniforms are fixed; card frames use saved school palettes. Local DM Sans and Barlow Condensed packages retain their upstream SIL Open Font License notices in node_modules and build output.
